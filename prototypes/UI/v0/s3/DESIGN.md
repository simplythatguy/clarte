# Style 3 · Stages

One screen per phase of the conversation, each with the fewest and largest targets that phase needs, and a real rest zone in every one. No page flip, no mode button: moving between stages is the mode.

Quadrant maps: [listen](quadrant-map-listen.svg) · [compose](quadrant-map-compose.svg) · [speak](quadrant-map-speak.svg) · [spoken](quadrant-map-spoken.svg).

## Stages

**Listen.** The top row rests; the partner's words arrive in the reading column (Q2–Q3), 44 px. Bottom row is one wide action, `done-listening` (Q5–Q8): **Done**. Any look at the bottom half for 900 ms ends listening. If that happens early, Listen can be reopened (Compose Q1) and keeps appending to the same partner turn; words spoken while not listening are not captured.

**Compose.** Q2 + Q3 rest with the partner's last turn, a hairline, and the message with the word in progress (typed glyphs in group ink, predicted rest as ghost letters). Q1 `accept`: the **candidate word** while groups are pending, **Done** when the message has words and nothing is pending, **Listen** when the message is empty. Q4 `delete`: **Delete**, or **Menu** when there is nothing to delete. Q5–Q8 `G1`–`G4`, each printing all of its letters.

*(Speak, Spoken and Menu changed while building; see D3 and D18. The quadrant map SVGs show the original plan.)*

**Speak.** Q2–Q3 rest with the whole message (52 px). Q1 `moment` **One moment** speaks the floor-holder; Q4 `menu` **Menu**; Q5 `edit` **Edit** returns to Compose with the message intact; Q7 + Q8 `speak` **Speak** reads it aloud with word highlighting, then moves to Spoken. Q6 rests. While speaking, the same screen keeps only `stop` (Q7 + Q8) **Stop**, the primary there. If speech fails, the screen returns to Speak with the failure said in the rest zone.

**Spoken.** Q2–Q3 rest with what was said, in the same place as on Speak. Q1 `listen` **Listen** (new turn, clears the message, as Q1 Listen on an empty Compose); Q4 `menu` **Menu**; Q5 `more` **Add more** (back to Compose keeping the message, where Edit is on Speak); Q7 + Q8 `again` **Say again**, in the slot where Speak was. Q6 rests.

**Menu.** Q2–Q3 rest with the page title; Q4 **Back**, the primary on every Menu page (the one way out). The root page holds Q1 **One moment**, Q5 **Recalibrate**, and three category entries that open their page directly: Q6 **Message** (Spell, Clear message, three saved phrases), Q7 **Selection** (letter and control speed, Select by, Letter repeat, Gaze noise), Q8 **Display and sound** (Text size, Theme, Sound, Word colours, Partner). Back on a category returns to the root; Back on the root returns to the stage Menu was opened from. Each setting tile shows its name small and its current value large; selecting it steps the value. Any setting is three selections from Compose, Speak or Spoken.

**Spell.** Letter-exact typing for names, opened from Menu → Message. Q1 `spell-add` shows the spelled word and adds it; Q2–Q3 rest with the word and a static caret; Q4 `spell-delete` **Delete** (or **Back** when empty); Q5–Q8 the four groups. Choosing a group shows its letters, at most four per page, one per bottom quadrant with the group's cap; Q1 offers the group's other letters (G1 E F, G2 K L M N, G3 S, G4 X Y Z) and Q4 **Back**. Two or three selections per letter.

One moment: Q1 on the Speak stage and Q1 of the Menu root; spoken automatically never. From Compose it is two selections (Done → One moment, or Menu → One moment on an empty message) and three with a word pending (accept it first), a known limit recorded in D3. The state band already tells the partner a reply is coming.

Fixed positions: a function has one home quadrant wherever it appears. Q1 is the way forward (the candidate, Done, Listen, Start, Add for Spell) or One moment where nothing moves forward; Q2–Q3 is where the person reads (message, what was said, Menu title, spelled name); Q4 is the way out (Delete, Menu, Back); Q5 returns to Compose from Speak and Spoken (Edit, Add more); Q7–Q8 is the voice (Speak, Stop, Say again); the groups always sit in Q5–Q8 on Compose and Spell.

## Selection

Default `mode: 'dwell'`, groups 600 ms, wide single actions and controls 900 ms. Stage changes happen only after a selection, never on a timer (the one exception is Speaking → Spoken when the voice finishes, the result of the Speak selection). The next stage's targets are inert for 400 ms after the transition, and every quadrant of the tile the deciding look was on stays inert until the look has been outside them for 200 ms, so the look that caused the change cannot select again (D4).

## Visual direction

*(Rewritten 2026-11-07 for the owner's shared skin, BRIEF "Visual direction" and MOTION.md. The earlier warm-paper direction is superseded; see "Redo decisions" R1–R16 below. Earlier decisions D1–D21 still hold where they concern layout and interaction.)*

The light finance-app look on apple.com neutrals: canvas `#F5F5F7`, white surfaces, ink `#1D1D1F`; cards that float in canvas inside their quadrant; pastel group faces with their group inks; the two-circle motif (a solid group-ink dot overlapping the dwell ring); one ink primary per screen; Satoshi 500/700; 40 px radii; thin 1.5 px outline icons on a 24 px grid; motion only when something happens, measured against the Neuralink calibration flow (MOTION.md).

## What this style tests

- Whether splitting the flow into stages (more screens, fewer targets each) is faster and calmer than one dense screen.
- Whether a Speak confirmation stage is worth its extra selection, against speaking directly from Compose.
- Whether the forward action staying in the same place across stages builds motor memory.

## Acceptance

All brief gates, plus: `clarteDebug.phases` lists every stage including menu pages; zero violations in each; the lockout after a stage change prevents an immediate re-selection (verify with the overlay); Listen reopened after Done appends to the transcript.

## Decisions made while building

Each is a choice the brief or this page left open, or a deliberate change to the text above, with its reason.

*Superseded by the redo (R1–R16 at the end): D1, D5, D6, D7, D8, D9, D10, D11, D15 and D20, which described the first warm-paper visual layer. D2, D3, D4, D12, D13, D14, D16, D17, D18, D19 and D21 still hold.*

**D1. No ink-filled primary tile.** The quadrant maps fill the primary with ink. Listen's primary spans Q5–Q8, so an ink tile there would drop mean screen luminance by about a third between Compose and Listen, against the luminance non-negotiable (R-1 §27, R-4 §18; "no luminance swings of 10% over large areas", R-4 §22). The primary is instead a deeper warm neutral (`#E2DACB` against `#ECE8E0` tiles) with a 700-weight label, and it always sits in the "forward" position. Measured mean luminance across every working screen stays within 7% (0.798–0.858); calibration screens, which have no tiles, are within 10% of the darkest working screen (`evidence/luminance.txt`). Council round 1 (Opus) suggested a pale chroma tint for the primary instead; declined because the four group hues and three state hues already occupy blue, green, purple, ochre, red, teal and violet, and a fifth pale hue would read as a group or a state. The primary keeps its place (Q1, or the Q7–Q8 voice slot), its deeper fill and the heaviest label.

**D2. Stage transitions are opacity only.** The 12 px rise in the original text moves content across a quadrant edge while the next targets are about to arm; the brief and R-6 §43 ask for phase changes "by opacity and colour in place". Exit 150 ms `ease-out`, enter 250 ms `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`, total 400 ms, the same as the lockout, so nothing can arm while anything changes. The state band changes colour in place (150 ms) at the moment of selection, before the screen fades, so the partner sees the new state first.

**D3. Menu and One moment on the Speak stage; one layout for Speak, Speaking and Spoken.** With words in the message, Compose's Q4 is Delete, so Menu and Recalibrate would otherwise be four or more selections away mid-message. The Speak stage takes Menu in Q4, the corner where Compose shows Menu, and One moment in Q1, where it is also the Menu root's first entry. Edit shrinks to Q5. Spoken reuses the same geometry (council round 1): Listen in Q1, Menu Q4, Add more Q5, Say again in the Speak slot Q7–Q8, and the sentence stays in Q2–Q3 on all three screens. Known limit: with a word pending, One moment is three selections (accept, Done, One moment), because Compose has no free quadrant without giving up half of the reading zone; the band already shows Composing to the partner. Both council members flagged this; giving One moment its own Compose quadrant would cost the rest zone the message needs at 48 px.

**D4. Lockout plus a fresh-look guard.** A 400 ms lockout alone does not stop the look that pressed a wide target from typing on the next screen: after Done (Q5–Q8) the eye is still there, and Compose has G1–G4 in those quadrants. So after a stage change caused by a look, or by the app while the person is looking (calibration Start, the end of speech), every quadrant of the deciding tile stays inert until gaze has been outside all of them, or lost, for 200 ms. A wide tile's centre sits on an internal grid line, so guarding one quadrant was not enough (council round 1 reproduced stray letters with noise on). Keys and clicks carry no guard. The lockout and the guard toggle a class with `inert`, because the shared tracker watches `class` but not `inert`; the lockout now measures 412–414 ms every time instead of 450–890 ms. Verified in `scripts/walk.mjs`.

**D5. Dwell rendering.** For the intent delay (150 ms groups, 250 ms controls) nothing shows. Then a 2 px track circle appears at 74% of the tile's shorter side, centred, ink at 56% (3.7:1 against the tile): the static focus cue and the visible end state. A disc in a 12% ink tint grows from the centre to meet the track; the label sits on top and never moves. At commit the disc is full and a 35 ms tick plays; on cancel the disc drains back over 150 ms. Under reduced motion the disc is full size from the start and its opacity ramps instead (a colour ramp, no size change, R-6 §3.6). Centred feedback keeps the eye on the tile centre (R-1 §16–19); a disc rather than a ring keeps the end state legible on wide tiles too.

**D6. Type.** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (Google Fonts), designed for low vision with distinct I, l, 1 and a large x-height (R-4 §14). Message 48/500, Speak 56/500, Listen transcript 44/400 (the partner's words lighter than one's own), group labels 32/600, control labels 28/600, primary labels 32/700, setting names 22/500, partner turn in Compose 26/400 secondary, state label 20/600. Text size scales everything to 200%; tile labels cap at the tile width so a long label never leaves its tile.

**D7. Geometry.** Half-gutter `clamp(25px, 1.95vw, 40px)` (56 px between tiles at 1440 × 900, 50 px at 1280 × 720); half row gap `clamp(32px, 4.5vh, 56px)` (81 px on the row boundary at 900 px tall, the widest gap, R-1 §25); 34 px top inset under the state band; radius 28 px on every tile. Tiles are flat fills with no outline: R-4 §3.5 bounds regions "by subtle hairlines or background steps", with 3:1 "only where a boundary is the sole identifier", and every tile carries a label at 11:1 or more. A 3:1 outline was tried and rejected: it read as a wireframe and outlines pull the eye to edges (R-5 §2.23).

**D8. Group identity.** A 10 px cap in the group colour on the tile's top edge, the printed range, and 1–4 pips under it (G1 one, G4 four), so identity never rests on colour (R-4 §1–4). Letter tiles in Spell keep their group's cap and pips.

**D9. Tokens and the candidate.** The pending word is drawn as its ghost candidate (secondary ink `#6B675F`, 5.0:1) on the message line, with its tokens hung directly beneath it like a ruby annotation: ink range label on a 14% group tint with a 5 px group bar. Space for the token row is always reserved, so accepting never shifts the message. On accept the ghost turns ink and the tokens fade out (180 ms, opacity). The Q1 tile shows the same candidate as its label: the tile names the action, the ghost lets the person read the sentence safely in the rest zone before choosing (R-1 §6: readable content outside dwell targets gave zero wrong selections). With one group and no exact-length word (most first groups), Q1 keeps its fill, shows no label and is not selectable: the slot is quiet when there is nothing to offer (R-2 §3.3), and the most prominent tile never changes surface mid-word.

**D10. State band.** 10 px bar across the top edge plus a centred 20 px label with a dot in the state colour: Listening `#B3261E` (red, the recording convention, 5.9:1), Composing `#0F7B8A` (teal, 4.5:1), Speaking `#5B3FA8` (violet, 6.9:1), Ready `#8A857C` (neutral, after speaking), Microphone off `#8A857C` (Listen when the microphone cannot listen). No colour in the band's label text; the dot and bar carry it. Hues avoid the four group hues' family where possible and the band is always at the top, the groups always at the bottom.

**D11. Speaking.** Words already spoken in ink, the current word in ink with a 6 px violet underline, words still to come in secondary ink: the partner can read along and see progress. Word timing comes from speech-synthesis boundary events, or a 360 ms-per-word timer when the browser sends none.

**D12. Listening.** Partner = Microphone when Web Speech exists, else Script; `?partner=script` or Menu → Display and sound → Partner picks the script explicitly. The microphone keeps listening until Done: Chrome ends continuous recognition after silence, so the app restarts it (`src/listen.ts`) and keeps every word heard. If the microphone cannot listen at all (no permission, no service, offline), the band says Microphone off and the rest zone says so with the fix; words are never invented in its place (council round 1 rejected the earlier silence-triggered script). Each scripted session continues the script, so reopened Listen appends a new line.

**D13. The mouse as gaze.** The shared pointer adapter samples only on movement, so a held mouse went stale after 600 ms and could never finish a 900 ms dwell. The app re-samples the last pointer position every frame (a held mouse is a steady look) and ticks the engine in the same loop. When the pointer leaves the window it feeds a missing sample every frame, so the engine's 200 ms blink grace expires on time and looking away cancels the dwell. Proposed for `shared/` in REPORT.md.

**D14. Two dwell classes on one engine.** The shared engine has one `dwellMs` and one `repeat`. The app sets both whenever a dwell starts: letter speed and the Letter repeat setting on group and letter tiles, control speed and `leave` on everything else, so "Keep looking" can repeat a letter but never a control. Proposed for `shared/` as per-action dwell and repeat.

**D15. Calibration.** On the page colour: an outlined face circle with "Sit so your face fills the circle"; nine 56 px outlined targets at the grid extremes (6/50/94% × 8/50/92%) with an 8 px centre dot; a 16 px ink guide dot travels between them (1200 ms move, 1800 ms settle, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`), and the active target fills ink with its centre dot inverted; four validation points at 28/72% (700 ms move, 1100 ms settle); then "Ready" with "Your looks landed well inside every tile." and a wide Start (Q5–Q8). Captions type at 38 ms per character with a static caret that fades out after typing. About 36 s; Escape skips. Under reduced motion the guide jumps instead of travelling.

**D16. Earcons.** Web Audio only: commit, a 35 ms 1760 Hz triangle tick; delete, a 70 ms falling blip 620→380 Hz; stage change, two soft notes 587 then 880 Hz. Off with Menu → Feedback → Sound. Browsers start audio only after a key press or click, so a mouse-only session is silent until then.

**D18. Menu shape.** A root with direct category entries instead of eight serial pages (council round 1): every setting is three selections from Compose, Speak or Spoken, against up to seven before. Settings show speeds in words (Fastest, Fast, Normal, Slow, Slower, Slowest), not milliseconds (R-1 §3, R-6 §15). A saved phrase chosen from Spoken starts a new message.

**D19. Failures are visible.** Speech reports ended, cancelled or failed. A failure (an engine error, or no end reported in time, after which synthesis is cancelled) returns to the screen Speak or Say again was chosen from, says "Speech did not play. Check the sound, then try again." in the rest zone with a small red dot, and plays a low two-note earcon a second later (BRIEF "Selection"). The next selection clears it.

**D20. Group colours on the real surfaces.** The P2 values were verified against white and `#121212`; on this page and these tiles G3 `#BB80C2` and G4 `#B38F39` measure 2.5–2.7:1, below the brief's 3:1 for group bars. Bars and tints use G3 `#A672AD` and G4 `#9F7F33` (each 11% toward black, 3.05–3.38:1 on page and tile); dark G1 `#676EAA` (11% toward white, 3.1:1 on the dark tile). Hues and their order are unchanged. Dark secondary ghost ink brightened to `#BDB8AF`.

**D21. Large text keeps whole lines.** Every rest zone that can overflow (Listen, Compose, Speak, Speaking, Spoken) shows a whole number of lines, newest line or the word being spoken in view. The token row wraps to the rest zone and slides left to stay inside it; tokens follow text size up to 150%. When the pending word and its tokens do not fit (200% on 1280 × 720), the partner's line steps aside first.

**D17. Declined generic polish.** (Still applies.) No press scale (better-ui's 0.96): nothing may move while a target is armed. No hover styles: the pointer is the gaze, and the dwell cue is the only feedback.

## Redo decisions (owner direction, 2026-11-07)

**R1. The skin.** Tokens exactly as the brief: canvas `#F5F5F7`, surface `#FFFFFF`, ink `#1D1D1F`, secondary `#6E6E73`, tertiary `#86868B`, hairline `#D2D2D7`, accent `#0071E3` on the dwell arc only, group faces and inks G1 `#E3F0FA`/`#1E5A82`, G2 `#ECE4F8`/`#59409A`, G3 `#E2F3EA`/`#2B6A4C`, G4 `#FBEADD`/`#8A4A22`. Faces kept at the brief's values (about 1.06:1 against canvas): a tile's extent is found by hue, and the dwelt tile's face steps one shade deeper as its focus cue (allowed by BRIEF "Colour", known limit). The status text uses `#636366` (5.4:1) because at 20 px the brief's `#6E6E73` (4.66:1) is marginal and Lint Apple caught it mid-fade at 4.45:1. D20's darkened P2 bars are retired with P2.

**R2. Cards, not stretched tiles.** Every single tile is a 40 px-radius card `25vw − 80 px` wide and `50vh − 128 px` tall, centred in its quadrant: 80 px between neighbours at 1440 × 900, 128 px across the row boundary (the widest gutter), 64 px of canvas at the screen edges. A wide action (Done listening, Start) is one 144 px-tall card spanning Q5–Q8 without touching their edges (it crosses x = 25% and 75% by 24 px, so it owns all four quadrants). A two-quadrant action (Speak, Stop, Say again) is a single card centred on the Q7/Q8 boundary, so it owns both while its ink area matches a single card (R9).

**R3. Centred composition.** The brief's hit-region rule asks for a centred label and MOTION's ring sits around it, so each card stacks its mark (motif or icon in ring) with the mark's bottom on the card's centre line and the label hanging 28 px below: marks and first baselines align across a row whatever the label length. At text sizes above 100% the whole cluster centres instead, so wrapped labels stay inside. The mock's top-left/bottom-left editorial composition was not copied: it puts the dwell ring in a corner, away from where the eye lands.

**R4. Letters, not ranges.** Group tiles print `groupLetters()` ("A B C D E F" …) in one tracked row, 32 px 700 at 1440 (28 px below 1360 px wide), group ink on group face, with a 1–4 dot count glyph beneath. At 150% text and above the row splits into two rows of at most four (explicit halves, not browser balancing), never below 24 px. Letter tiles in Spell show one 64 px letter.

**R5. The word in progress.** `pendingGlyphs()`: typed glyphs in their group's ink, the predicted rest as ghost letters in tertiary ink, one dot per typed group when no candidate exists. A new glyph appears by opacity over 150 ms (`@starting-style`); a deleted glyph fades out in place over 120 ms on an absolutely positioned copy, so nothing slides; an accepted word's letters settle from group ink to ink over 180 ms, colour only. Word colours keeps group inks on accepted words.

**R6. The dwell indicator.** Two circles: a 52 px solid dot in group ink overlapping a 64 px ring (group tiles), or the ring around a 28 px outline icon (controls). The 1.5 px track is always there at 30%. For 150 ms (groups) or 250 ms (controls) nothing changes; then the face steps one shade and the track goes opaque (150 ms, `--ease-state`); the 3 px accent arc draws clockwise from 12 o'clock, linear, over exactly the dwell remaining. The arc is two half-ring blades in clipped halves rotated with the Web Animations API, so it runs on the compositor; a cancel plays the same animations backwards to empty within 150 ms (the drain); a commit finishes them (the solid ring), plays the 35 ms tick and blooms a copy of the ring 1 → 2.4× while fading .35 → 0 over 450 ms `--ease-out`, transform and opacity only. While gaze stays the ring stays solid; when it leaves the ring fades to its track over 150 ms. Reduced motion: no arc and no bloom; the track's opacity steps .3 → 1 in four steps across the dwell.

**R7. Nothing relabels under a look.** A tile keeps the content it had when the look arrived until the look leaves (MOTION "Word accepted"; HIG "freeze the candidate under an armed dwell"), then cross-fades its new label in place over 150 ms, old and new stacked in one grid cell. Settings are the exception: their new value is the selection's feedback.

**R8. Stage change: one cross-fade in place.** The outgoing layer stays the same DOM element and fades 1 → 0 over 150 ms `--ease-out`; the incoming layer fades 0 → 1 over 300 ms `--ease-state` starting at 100 ms; the 400 ms lockout covers both. Opacity only, measured frame by frame (`evidence/demo-transition.txt`). The band and status change colour in place at the moment of selection. Keyboard and click actions skip all of this: state changes at once (MOTION "No motion at all on keyboard-triggered actions").

**R9. One ink primary, constant luminance.** The primary is the ink card the brief asks for. To keep mean luminance flat the ink area is the same on every screen: one card, or the shorter wide card; Menu pages make Back the primary (the one exit) and Speaking makes Stop the primary (the only action). Measured: working screens 0.820–0.872 (6%); the calibration canvas, which has no cards, is 10.3% brighter than Compose (`evidence/luminance.txt`).

**R10. State.** A 4 px band at the top edge in the state colour only while the partner is involved: red `#D70015` while recording, ink while speaking. The status field above the reading column (an 8 px dot in the state colour and a 20 px label) shows every state: Listening, Microphone off, Composing, Speaking, Ready. State colours form a luminance ladder (red, ink, grey) so they survive colour-blindness; the accent blue never means state.

**R11. Type.** Satoshi 500/700 from Fontshare. Message, sentence and spelled name 52/700 −0.025em, line-height 1.12; menu title 44/700; transcript 44/500 −0.015em; partner 26/500 secondary; control labels 38/700 −0.02em; primary word 44/700; setting name 20/500 and value 28/700; status 20/500; captions 20/500. Every size scales with Text size up to 200% and is capped by the card's width and height (size container queries), and a label sizes to its longest word so it never breaks inside a word. All sizes on Lint Apple's scale at 100% (`evidence` lint summary in REPORT.md).

**R12. Icons.** One set, `src/icons.tsx`: 24 px grid, 1.5 px stroke, round caps and joins, `currentColor`. Every control and setting has one, inside its ring.

**R13. Calibration to MOTION.** Position: a 1.5 px ring 38% of the shorter side, caption "Sit so your face fills the circle." with "circle" highlighted; after the 1.2 s simulated check the ring turns accent with a 12% fill and "Good." types below; 600 ms beat. Nine 28 px outlined targets fade in together; "Follow the dot." types; the 10 px guide dot travels 1200 ms on `--ease-move`; during the 1800 ms settle the target shows a 6 px dot and the same compositor arc fills at the dwell ring's rate; bloom 450 ms; 400 ms beat. Check: four points, 900/1200 ms, "One more look.". Ready: title, result, the Start card. Captions are white pills (1 px hairline, radius 12, 10 × 14 padding) typing at 32 ms per character with 180 ms after punctuation, keyword in a darker accent on its 14% tint (4.5:1), the previous line settling to secondary. About 48 s; Escape skips.

**R14. Large text and long words.** Text windows keep whole lines with 8 px of padding inside the clip so ascenders and accents are never cut (Lint Apple found a 4 px clip). Worst-case content (`?data=worst`: a long partner turn, "uncomfortable", a 16-word reply, a long spelled name) holds at both viewports at 100% and 200% (`scripts/worst.mjs`). Known limit: at 200% on a 720 px-tall screen the word in progress may show only its last line.

**R15. Performance.** During a dwell the main thread does no layout and no paint for the ring (the arc is compositor-only); the per-frame style ticks in the trace come from the gaze sampler's `requestAnimationFrame` loop, which forces a main frame every frame, not from the ring (a bare test div ticks the same way inside the app and not on a blank page). Focus step and commit are the only paints (`evidence/perf-dwell.txt`).

**R16. Two dwell-ring forms for the record.** MOTION asks for the ring around the label; the visual direction's motif puts a ring beside a solid dot. On group tiles the ring is the second circle of the motif (beside the dot, above the letters) because a ring around an eight-letter row would be a 280 px second surface; it still sits within 40 px of the card's centre, which is what R-1 §16–19 asks of a progress cue.

### Changes after council round 2 (Opus 7.5/7, Sol 7/6.5)

- **R2 revised.** A two-quadrant action (Speak, Stop, Say again) is now a wide 144 px card spanning Q7–Q8 (`50vw − 80 px`), the same form as Done: a look at the right of Q8 fired Speak while that area looked like bare canvas (Opus). Its ink area still matches one card.
- **R8 revised.** A true cross-fade: both layers run the same 300 ms `--ease-state` curve from the same frame. (Their opacities sum to one, but stacked layers composite to a + (1 − a)·a, so up to a quarter of the canvas shows through at the midpoint; both layers are mostly canvas and card faces of near-equal luminance, so the measured dip stays small.) MOTION's staggered 150/300 ms dipped through bare canvas and brightened the screen by 10% on every stage change (Opus, measured); the brief's luminance rule outranks it. Measured now: within 0.8–3.7% of the end states, never brighter (`evidence/flash.txt`; the remaining dip is gamma-space blending of ink areas).
- **R6 revised.** The face step is an overlay whose opacity changes and the track's dim/opaque state is the SVG's own opacity, both composited; the primary card no longer lightens on focus, which kept the accent arc at 3.6:1 instead of 2.5:1 (Sol). Ring 56 px, motif dot 36 px (Opus: the 52 px dot outweighed the letters). Under reduced motion the commit turns the track accent and release fades it back over 150 ms (Opus). With Letter repeat on, the next repeat counts down visibly after the commit cue (Sol).
- **R4 revised.** Letter rows 30 px at 1440 (28 px under 1360 px wide), leaving about 25 px to the card edges.
- **R7 extended.** Accept commits exactly what the frozen tile shows: the decoder context is captured when the look arrives, and recognition results that arrive after Listen has ended no longer change the context (Sol reproduced "Was" shown, "Yes" accepted).
- **R9 revised.** Calibration (position, points, check) sits on `#EBEBEF`, one step darker than the canvas, so its mean luminance matches the working screens; every screen including calibration is now within 5.9% (`evidence/luminance.txt`).
- **R10 revised.** The 6 px band shows every state (red listening, grey composing, ink speaking, light grey ready), because composing is when partners most misjudge silence (Opus). The status label is hidden on Menu pages so nothing sits above the page title.
- **R11 revised.** 200% now enlarges controls to the limit of the card: labels are bounded only by their longest word and the card's height (Sol measured 20 → 25.6 px before). Dark theme gets its own group text inks for glyphs on the canvas (≥ 7:1; Sol measured 2.3–2.8:1).
