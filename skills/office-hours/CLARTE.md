## Premise challenge, Clarte questions

Add these to Phase 3:

- **Who is the user in this moment?** The person speaking through Clarte, the partner across the table, or someone helping set it up. Never infer abilities or wishes from a diagnosis.
- **What can go wrong when speech is involved?** Words that can't be unheard, a reply the person didn't choose, a voice made without consent, a partner recorded without being told.
- **Does it keep the gaze contract?** One action per backend quadrant, rest zones, nothing happening on its own, constant luminance.
- **Does it stay on the device?**
- **What evidence would show it works?** A recorded session, a measured rate, a real conversation, not a synthetic fixture.
